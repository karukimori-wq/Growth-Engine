# Business integration guardrail

Professional app integrations do not imply Business access.

Until Business is formally offered, cross-app Business functionality stays closed. Free and Pro users must not gain Business capabilities through Numeria Studio handoff, Velvet Customer integration, Velvet Visit integration, endpoint configuration, or domain changes.

Future Business access requires the shared plan contract to identify `business`, the Business offering to be available, and the Business feature flag to be enabled. Existing Free/Pro integration behavior must continue working independently of Business availability.
